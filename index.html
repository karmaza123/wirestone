Wirestone

Sketch it on a tablet. Ship it as a web page.

Wirestone is a drawing app for Android tablets and the S Pen. Sketch, paint, design vector shapes and build app wireframes in one place, then export your screens as real HTML.

Coming soon to Google Play

From wireframe to web page

Pick a phone, tablet or desktop screen and drag ready-made Material 3 blocks onto it. Move them, resize them, edit their text. One tap exports a responsive HTML page that matches what you drew.

Draw like a designer

Vector strokes stay sharp at any zoom and stay editable. Raster pages paint pixels like real paper. The Pen tool gives you Bézier curves you can reshape point by point.

Everything in reach

Built around the S Pen, with your tools on a wheel that follows your hand.

83 brushes

Inking, airbrush, blur and smudge, halftone screen tones, texture, noise and grunge stamps, plus watercolor, oil and acrylic paints whose bristles streak and mix on the page.

Tool wheel

Spin it to your favourite brushes; eraser, undo and redo under your thumb.

SnapShape

Hold the pen at the end of a stroke and circles, rectangles and stars snap clean.

Colour that fits

Harmonies, 14 subject libraries from skin tones to sunsets, and saved colours.

Layers and precision

Opacity, blend modes, layer lock, photo import to paint over, free selection that keeps paint inside an outline, mirror painting, guides and measuring.

Any page size

Screen, Full HD, 4K, A4, A5, Instagram or custom, with autosave and a gallery.

Try the alpha

Wirestone 0.9.8 alpha for Android tablets (Android 10 or newer). Draw with the S Pen or a finger; best with a pen, like the Galaxy Tab S10 FE. New: your own stamp spacing for every brush, free selection (draw an outline and paint stays inside it), real bristle oil and acrylic brushes, and texture, noise and grunge stamp brushes. Already have an earlier alpha? Install this over it; your drawings stay. From now on the app shows a blue Update button when a new version is out.
